An international academic background

Emanuel Namaiwa Barke is a PhD candidate in Economics jointly supervised by Université Marie et Louis Pasteur (CRESE, France) and the University of Sousse (LAMIDED, Tunisia). Originally from Niger, he studied at the Lycée d’excellence de Niamey and in Sousse, and took part in an Erasmus+ exchange in Nice. He received the Eiffel Excellence scholarship (2024–2026) and works in industrial organization and theoretical microeconomics. He has held an ATER teaching and research position in Besançon since September 2026.
